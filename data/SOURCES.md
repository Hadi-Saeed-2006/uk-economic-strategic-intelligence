### Important interpretation note

The dataset mixes annual measures with clearly labelled latest reference periods because some official indicators are published as rolling three-month estimates or point-in-time observations. The dashboard should therefore display the reference period alongside any metric used for decision-making.

The 2020-2024 historical rows are retained as a development baseline and should be progressively replaced with a consistent vintage from the official source datasets before publication as a research-grade historical series.

## Primary institutions

- Office for National Statistics (ONS): GDP, CPI, labour market, earnings, trade and public finances.
- Bank of England (BoE): Bank Rate and sterling exchange rates.

## Modelling

Opportunity, pressure and strategic-balance scores are model-derived outputs. They are not official statistics and should be interpreted as an analytical framework rather than a forecast.
